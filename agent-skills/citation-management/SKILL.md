# Citation Management Skill

Use this skill to attach source references to the claims they actually support.

## Principles

1. Put citations near the supported claim.
2. Do not cite a source for claims it does not support.
3. Prefer the smallest source range that proves the point.
4. Keep source identity stable through retrieval and generation.
5. Avoid citation dumping where many sources are attached without clear claim mapping.

## Workflow

```text
Draft claim
  ↓
Identify supporting evidence
  ↓
Validate source scope
  ↓
Attach citation
  ↓
Check citation after editing
```

## Citation drift

After revisions, verify that citations still support the exact nearby claims. Moving or combining sentences can silently break source alignment.

## Contradictory sources

When reliable sources disagree, cite the competing evidence and explain the disagreement rather than selecting one silently.

## Missing citation

If a material claim cannot be traced to evidence in a grounded task, either retrieve more evidence, qualify the claim, or remove it.
